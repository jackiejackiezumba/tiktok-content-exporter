TikTok Content Exporter

TikTok Content Exporter is a desktop application designed to help users retrieve available video information from their own TikTok accounts, select videos, and export the selected data to spreadsheet files for reporting and analysis.

How it works

  1. Connect your TikTok account through the authorized login process.
  2. Retrieve the available video information permitted by TikTok.
  3. Select the videos and data you want to export.
  4. Export the selected data to a spreadsheet.
